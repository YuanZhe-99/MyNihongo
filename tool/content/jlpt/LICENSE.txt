A note on corrections
---------------------

These files are kept byte-identical to upstream. Three rows point at a JMdict
homograph rather than the word the list means; the corrections live in
jlptSeqCorrections in tool/src/vocab_import_core.dart, next to the reason for
each one, rather than being edited into the data here.
